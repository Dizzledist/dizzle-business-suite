## Structured invoice parsing and PDFs

`lib/zoho.ts` (`zoho`) uses server-side OAuth and scopes each request to the configured Inventory organization. `lib/sync.ts` (`sync`) walks descending pages of `GET /invoices`, obtains `GET /invoices/{id}`, and maps structured `line_items`. It prefers the source SKU, then a stored item-ID-to-SKU lookup, then `GET /items/{id}` when a SKU is still missing. It takes the name from the line name, description, or an unnamed label and converts `quantity` to a number. It retains item ID, SKU, name, description, unit, and `invoice_number:line_item_id`. It retains the final invoice total and current status, but the stored costing lines lack unit selling prices, discounts and line totals. The assistant's `invoice_details` separately returns `item_total` and tries `GET /invoices/{id}/payments`. `customersByProduct` uses `rate × quantity` only as a fallback to test whether a line has positive value. `productMatches` is specific to search, separate from SKU cost matching. [Inventory client](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/zoho.ts) · [Invoice sync and line projection](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/sync.ts#L7) · [Assistant product and invoice reads](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/assistant-data.ts#L21)

`lib/ocr.ts` (`extractInvoice`) sends the PDF to Azure Document Intelligence's prebuilt invoice model when configured, checks that the polling URL has the same origin, and polls up to 20 times. `sync` stores the OCR result separately and marks failures for retry while the structured import continues. Provider failures reject the sync and its lease is released. `app/api/invoices/[id]/route.ts` (`GET`) serves the current PDF after numeric ID validation and returns a generic failure on read errors. The route itself has no CRM employee or tenant guard. Business Suite keeps its authenticated and invoice-scoped PDF viewer and its current structured parser, which preserves decimal strings, selling prices, discounts and line amounts. [OCR adapter](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/ocr.ts) · [Management PDF route](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/app/api/invoices/%5Bid%5D/route.ts)

Business Suite now reuses the Management name fallback in the invoice line display. The reusable cost utility also accepts an explicitly supplied item-to-SKU lookup for lines missing a SKU. It does not change a stored Zoho SKU or historical snapshot. The original CRM parser remains authoritative for structured identity, price and discount fields. The test compares the legacy projection and CRM structured record using the same input, including a source SKU that required an item lookup in Management. [Management line projection](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/sync.ts#L9)

## Profit calculation and dependencies

- `lib/cost-csv.ts` (`parseCostCsv`) accepts SKU, Zoho Item ID, product name, nonnegative purchase price, and optional exact invoice-line references. It rejects malformed, duplicate or ambiguous identities. `app/api/costs/route.ts` (`POST`) verifies exact invoice lines against structured Zoho product details, writes the approved CSV rows to Postgres, removes ambiguous SKU lookups, then recalculates previously stored invoices. Tables defined by `lib/costs.ts` (`costTables`) are `invoice_line_costs` by exact line reference, `item_costs` by Zoho item ID, `products` by SKU, and `named_costs` by a product identity key. None of those four costing tables has an invoice-currency or effective-date column. [CSV parser](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/cost-csv.ts) · [Cost import](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/app/api/costs/route.ts) · [Cost tables](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/costs.ts)
- `lib/costs.ts` (`decodeLines`, `calculateCost`) decodes the stored array, then takes the first available unit cost from exact invoice line, item ID, SKU, or exact normalized name, description and unit if both item ID and SKU are missing. `lib/product-match.ts` (`productMatchKey`) trims, lowercases and collapses whitespace in those three fields. Invoice cost is `Math.round(sum(quantity × unitCost) × 100) ÷ 100`. Zero cost is valid. An empty list, missing match, or invalid or negative quantity makes the invoice cost unknown. There is no separate product-level profit allocation. [Cost computation](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/costs.ts#L4) · [Match key](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/product-match.ts)
- `app/ui.tsx` (`Dashboard`) computes invoice profit as sales less invoice cost. Margin is profit divided by invoice sales times 100, with zero shown when sales is zero for a costed invoice. Summary profit is the sum across costed invoices. Its weighted margin divides that sum by the sales of the costed subset. Uncosted invoices still contribute to sales and counts. Discounts are already reflected in Management's final total. There is no separate allocation of tax, shipping expense, invoice-level discounts, overhead, credits or refunds to product profit. [Profit formulas](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/app/ui.tsx#L12) · [Invoice presentation](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/app/ui.tsx#L24)

The pure Business Suite utilities `productMatchKey`, `calculateManagementInvoiceCost` and `summarizeManagementProfit` in `server/sales-crm/reporting/management-profit.ts` preserve the cost order, one rounding step per invoice, unknown-cost treatment and weighted-margin denominator. The caller explicitly supplies a scoped catalog and currency. The cost utility rejects a currency mismatch and invalid catalog amounts. This is an added validation rule for the adaptation. The formula still uses Management's numeric arithmetic for parity and requires approved production precision and cost history rules. No production cost values were read and no live reporting path invokes the utilities.

## Sample tracking findings

`lib/sample-parser.ts` contains `sampleKey`, `isSampleName`, `parseSampleDescription`, `sampleEntriesFromLine`, `currentSampleMonth` and `sampleMonthBounds`. It selects lines whose **name** contains the word sample or samples. A generic line named Sample or Samples expands its description by newline. A prefix such as `2 x Product` or suffix such as `Product × 2` is multiplied by the parent invoice-line quantity. Named sample products use their line quantity directly. Unparseable or invalid quantities remain unresolved. Ordinary paid lines on mixed invoices are excluded unless the line name meets the sample rule. [Sample parser](https://github.com/Dizzledist/dizzle-management/blob/f8da43c294545cd3f04bcc063fc9c84ad64223c3/lib/sample-parser.ts)

`lib/samples.ts` (`sampleTables`, `syncSamples`, `sampleDashboard`, `readEntries`) stores entries under a source invoice ID in `sample_orders`, with the invoice number, date, rep, currency and modified timestamp. There is no foreign key to the profit invoice mirror. Costs prefer an explicit `sample_unit_costs` rule keyed by normalized name and currency. USD may fall back to a single distinct cost for an exact normalized catalog title in `item_costs` or `named_costs`. A known line cost is quantity times sample-unit cost rounded to cents. Unknown lines stay pending and the UI sums only known costs while showing unresolved counts. Saved rules reprice matching samples in that currency. `app/api/samples/sync/route.ts` and `app/api/samples/costs/route.ts` expose the existing Management operations. `app/samples/page.tsx` (`Page`) and `app/samples/ui.tsx` (`SampleCost`) present the monthly view. Samples integration remains deferred. ## Parity and regression results

The fixture capture executed the pinned Management `sync`, filters, cost calculations, dashboard formulas and sample parser using synthetic invoices, a fake database and fake Zoho reads. `scripts/capture-management-parity.mjs` recreates the checked-in reference output from a clean clone at the audited revision. The tests use that output and run the same input through Business Suite. Management sample results are part of the source capture only.

| Synthetic October selection | Management count | Management final totals | CRM count | CRM recognized subtotals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All | 4 | USD 414.00 | 3 | USD 300.00 |
| Open | 3 | USD 288.00 | 2 | USD 200.00 |
| Closed | 1 | USD 126.00 | 1 | USD 100.00 |

The CRM includes the September-dated October send and holds the two October rows lacking verified recognition. The closed invoice demonstrates that the current final total, current subtotal and original subtotal differ. For identical selected rows and revenue inputs, the adapted cost and profit outputs matched Management across all three selections. Tests also cover exact override, item and SKU priority, missing-cost treatment, invoice rounding, description fallback, catalog fallback without rewriting a source SKU, quantity and explicit discounts. The existing October coverage, password and tenant authorization, Zoho read-only and warehouse checks ran in the same regression suites. TypeScript and build passed. Vitest passed 224 checks across 16 files, including six parity cases. Chromium passed 27 checks across four isolated modes, split 4 with CRM disabled, 9 with fixture CRM, 7 with session-only auth and 7 with password auth.
